What the desktop app adds

A window of its own

No tabs, no browser shortcuts stealing your keys. Every hotkey lands in the editor.

Works offline

The editor ships inside the app. Open it on a plane and keep charting.

Same account, same maps

Sign in with osu! and your cloud maps, collabs and comments follow you between the browser and the desktop.

More room to chart

Without browser chrome eating the top of the screen, the playfield gets the space back.

Before you install

Windows will warn you once

The builds are not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen shows “Windows protected your PC”. Choose More info, then Run anyway.

WebView2 comes along

The installers fetch Microsoft’s WebView2 runtime when it is missing. Windows 11 already has it, and the portable exe needs it too.

Updating

Download the newer build and run it over the old one. Your settings and local maps stay where they are.

macOS and Linux

Not yet. Until then Cascade runs in any current browser on those systems.

Not on Windows?

Cascade runs in any modern browser with the same editor, the same cloud maps and nothing to install.

Cascade

Convert osu!mania, StepMania, Etterna, Quaver and Malody charts online

Cascade opens charts from five vertical scrolling rhythm games and saves them for any of the others, directly in your browser. Open an osu!mania .osz, a StepMania or Etterna .sm, a Quaver .qua or a Malody .mcz, check it on the playfield, and export it in the format you need. There is nothing to install, and the conversion runs on your own machine.

How to convert a chart

  1. Open Cascade and drag your file onto the page: an .osz, .osu, .sm, .ssc, .qua, .mc or .mcz. For a single chart without its song, drop the audio file too.
  2. Check the chart: play it back, and fix any notes, holds, BPM changes or offsets before you export.
  3. Open the File menu and pick Export .osz, Export .osu, Export .sm, Export .qua or Export .mcz.

What carries over

  • Notes and long notes (holds), from 1K up to 10K and beyond where the target game allows it
  • BPM changes, time signatures and the song offset
  • Scroll speed changes (SV) for osu!mania, Quaver and Malody
  • Title, artist, mapper and difficulty names, including original-language titles for osu! and Malody
  • The audio, background image and preview point, in every format that stores them

What a game has no place for stays behind: StepMania and Malody have no osu!-style hitsounds, Quaver and Malody have no OD or HP, and Malody keysounds are not imported. Each export keeps everything the target format can hold.

osu!mania to StepMania and Etterna

Export .sm downloads a zipped StepMania song folder with the audio and background bundled, which Etterna reads too. StepMania header fields such as #SUBTITLE, #GENRE and #DISPLAYBPM can be edited in Map Settings. Rate-changed difficulties are left out, because Etterna applies rates in game.

osu!mania to Malody, and back

Export .mcz packs every difficulty into one Malody set, with each chart placed on Malody's beat grid so notes keep clean snaps. Opening a Malody .mcz brings every Key mode chart in with its song and background, ready to export as an .osz for osu!mania.

osu!mania to Quaver, and back

Export .qua saves the difficulty you are editing as a Quaver map, with its scroll speed changes and bookmarks. Opening a .qua also applies that map's scroll setting, so SV and BPM gimmicks look the way they do in Quaver.

Why not a script?

Offline converter scripts exist, but they give you no way to check the result. Cascade shows the converted chart on a scrolling playfield with audio, so you can play it back, fix timing or patterns, and only then export. It doubles as a full online osu!mania editor and an osu!mania map viewer.

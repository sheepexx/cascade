Cascade

An osu!mania mapping Discord: overdosu!

overdosu! is an osu!mania community for players and mappers, and the home of Cascade on Discord. Come find someone to map with, show off what you've made, and talk osu!mania with people who play and map it.

Join the Discord

Find collab and GD partners

Looking for a guest difficulty for your set, or a mapper to share a collab with? Ask in the server. Once you've found someone, Cascade lets you map the same chart together in real time instead of passing .osz files back and forth.

Share your maps

Post the maps you've finished, ranked or not, and see what others are mapping. A Map Card turns a difficulty's star rating, MSD skillsets and note counts into one image you can paste straight into Discord.

Talk osu!mania

Patterns, skins, songs you want to map, the latest ranked sets: the server is a place to talk osu! and osu!mania with other players and mappers.

Cascade's home on Discord

New versions are announced in #cascade-updates. Bugs and feature ideas go in the Cascade forum, where the people making Cascade read and answer them, and you can talk to them directly.

The Cascade bot

  • /profile and /stats show a Cascade profile with its mapping time, notes placed and maps edited, as far as the profile shows them to everyone
  • /leaderboard ranks the mappers who choose to appear, and everyone's contributor points
  • /bug and /suggest post your report to the forum for you. Accepted ones earn contributor points, and enough points give the Contributor role and a badge on your Cascade profile
  • /roadmap and /changelog show what's planned and what's new

One account for both

You can log in to Cascade with Discord, or connect Discord to the account you already have in Settings, Account. /link in the server takes you there.

Questions

Is there a Discord for osu!mania mapping?
Yes. overdosu! is an osu!mania community where mappers find collab and GD partners, share their maps and talk osu!mania. It's also Cascade's official Discord.
Do I need to use Cascade to join?
No. Players and mappers are welcome whichever editor they map in.
Can I find a guest difficulty mapper there?
Yes. Ask in the server for a GD or a collab partner, then map together in Cascade or swap files as usual.
Is it free?
Yes. The Discord server is free, and so is Cascade.

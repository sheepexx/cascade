Cascade and ArrowVortex compared

ArrowVortex is a free Windows editor for StepMania simfiles, and the tool many StepMania and Etterna charters already know. Cascade is a free editor built around osu!mania that runs in the browser and as a desktop app, and it also opens and saves StepMania files. They overlap, but they are built for different games, so the better choice depends on what you chart for.

When ArrowVortex is the better tool

If you chart for StepMania itself, stay with ArrowVortex. StepMania has note types osu!mania doesn't, such as mines, rolls, lifts and fakes, and Cascade can't place them. Opening a simfile in Cascade turns rolls into ordinary holds and leaves mines, lifts and fakes out, and Cascade saves .sm but not .ssc.

When Cascade may suit you better

  • You map for osu!mania first: scroll velocity, hitsounds, osu! export and the AiMod-style checks are built in.
  • You're on macOS, Linux or a Chromebook: there are desktop builds for macOS and Linux, and the editor also runs in the browser with nothing to install.
  • You want one chart in several games: the same map saves as .osu, .sm, Quaver .qua and Malody .mcz (see converting to StepMania, Quaver and Malody).
  • You chart for Etterna: Cascade shows each difficulty's MSD skillset ratings while you map.
  • You map with others: live sessions let several people edit one map at once, and you can playtest it in the browser.

Moving a chart from StepMania to Cascade

  1. Drop the .sm or .ssc (with its song) onto Cascade.
  2. Taps and holds come across as they are. BPM changes become red lines, and stops are kept by shifting the notes after them. Rolls become holds; mines, lifts and fakes are left out.
  3. Check the timing with the BPM and offset tools if the simfile's offset looks off.

Saving back to StepMania

Cascade writes .sm for 4, 5, 6, 7, 8 and 10 keys. Notes are placed on StepMania's grid of 192 steps per beat, so a note can move by a millisecond or two. Scroll velocity and hitsounds have no place in a .sm and are left out, and the check before export tells you about any difficulty with a key count StepMania can't hold.

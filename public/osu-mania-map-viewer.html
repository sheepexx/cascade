Cascade

Preview osu!mania maps online

Want to see a beatmap without launching osu!? Cascade is a free osu!mania map viewer that runs in the browser: drop an .osz onto the page and the map plays on a vertical scrolling playfield with the song, real osu! hitsounds, background image or video, and even your own .osk skin.

Open the map viewer

How it works

  1. Open Cascade and drag any .osz file onto the window.
  2. Press Space to play. Scrub the timeline, change playback speed (25% to 100%), or jump between bookmarks and kiai sections.
  3. Switch difficulties in the sidebar to compare charts in the same set.

Viewer features

  • Every keymode from 1K to 18K, with long notes, SVs and BPM changes rendered accurately
  • Playtest mode: play the map with your keyboard, with judgements, combo and accuracy
  • Waveform timeline, per-difficulty star rating and a PP counter
  • Skin support: load an .osk to see the map with your own note style
  • Slow-motion playback for reading dense patterns

More than a viewer

Cascade is a full online osu!mania editor: the same page lets you fix a note, retime a section, or convert the map to StepMania / Etterna and export it back out as .osu, .osz or .sm. If you are new to mapping, start with the guide on how to make an osu!mania map.
